# TrustLock User Study — Your Task List

Thanks for helping test TrustLock, an escrow platform for online buying
and selling. No real money is involved anywhere in this session — the
payment card and everything else below is a test setup.

As you go, please talk through what you're thinking, even if something
seems obvious or you get stuck. There's no wrong way to do this — we're
testing the app, not you.

Your facilitator will tell you which track below applies to you.

---

## Everyone starts here

Create your account:

- **If you're on an email track:** sign up with an email and password.
- **If you're on a wallet track:** click "Continue with wallet" and
  connect via MetaMask instead. This needs either a laptop with the
  MetaMask browser extension, or a phone with the MetaMask app — if
  using a phone, open this study from inside the MetaMask app's own
  browser, not your phone's regular browser. Your facilitator will help
  make sure this is set up before you start.

Once your facilitator confirms your track, follow the matching list
below.

---

## Track A — Email, Buyer

1. Create a transaction by inviting a counterparty by email (your
   facilitator will give you an email to use).
2. Once it's accepted, fund the transaction — choose **Transfer** as
   the payment method (more reliable in testing than Card). If your
   facilitator asks you to try Card instead: test card
   `4084084084084081`, any future expiry date, CVV `408`, PIN `0000`,
   OTP `123456`, and select **Success** on the option shown before
   paying.
3. Before doing anything else, find and read the platform fee and when
   you'd expect the seller to get paid.
4. Go to **Marketplace** in the sidebar, pick anything listed, and buy
   it directly.
5. Raise a dispute on either transaction and look at what options are
   offered to resolve it.
6. Find another user's public profile and look at their rating,
   completed deals, and verification badges.

## Track B — Email, Seller

1. Go to **Marketplace → Sell something** and publish a listing —
   title, description, a photo if you'd like, price, and quantity.
2. Once someone buys and funds it, find it under **Dispatch products**
   and mark it shipped.
3. Create a second transaction, this time the direct way — invite
   someone by email rather than through a listing.
4. Go to **Payments** in the sidebar and add payout/bank account
   details. Look at what shows up in the payout history.
5. Find another user's public profile and look at their reputation.

## Track C — Wallet, Buyer

Same steps as Track A. One thing to pay attention to: when you get to
funding a transaction, check the "Email for receipt" field carefully
before submitting.

## Track D — Wallet, Seller

Same steps as Track B.
